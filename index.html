About Juma

Juma M. Wekesa is a Kenyan author, public speaker, youth mentor, and youth advocate passionate about leadership, education, personal development, and empowering young people to discover and fulfil their potential.

Through writing, public speaking, and mentorship, he seeks to inspire a generation of responsible, confident, and purpose-driven leaders.

His Vision

A generation of responsible, confident, and purpose-driven leaders.

“Every opportunity comes with a responsibility.” From Unleashing the Hero in Youth
